Text Instructions

1

Open Instagram Settings

Tap your profile picture, then tap the menu (☰) and choose Settings.

2

Go to Settings and privacy

In the menu, select Settings and privacy.

3

See more in Accounts Center

Scroll down and tap See more in Accounts Center.

4

Your information and permissions

In Accounts Center, tap Your information and permissions.

5

Export your information

Tap Export your information.

6

Create Export

Tap Create export to start the process.

7

Export to device

Choose Export to device.

8

Select Range and Format

Set Range to All time and Format to JSON, then confirm.

9

Start Export

Tap Start export to begin downloading your data.

10

Wait for Instagram's Email

Instagram will email you when your file is ready. This can take 2-15 minutes. Once you receive it, download the file and upload it here to see who's fake!

Bonus: Faster Download

Want a faster download? Only select "Followers and Following" in the info selection screen. Need help? Watch our tutorial!
